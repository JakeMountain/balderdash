# Word list

About 250 obscure words whose real definitions are already gross, cheeky, or absurd, so a silly bluff doesn't stand out. Definitions are written casually so the real one sounds like everyone else's fake.

How to play in person: the dealer picks a word, spells it, and says its part of speech. Everyone writes a fake while the dealer copies the real one onto a slip in their own handwriting. The dealer shuffles and reads them all in the same deadpan voice. If anyone already knows the word, pick another.

Bits in parentheses are notes for the dealer; skip them when reading aloud. The Spicy section is grouped so you can skip it around the in-laws.
